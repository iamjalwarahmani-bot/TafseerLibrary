A Gentle Note for the Reader

These Roman Urdu notes have been thoughtfully selected and simplified from Tafseer Sirat-ul-Jinan, with the aim of making the understanding of each Surah easier and more accessible. Difficult words and concepts have been presented in a simple way so that the reading remains clear, gentle, and easy to follow.

Each Surah has been arranged in an organised manner, allowing you to return to any Surah whenever you wish and continue your reading at your own pace.

If you have come across these notes through someone sharing this website with you, you are most welcome to read them and benefit from them in whatever way is helpful to you.

May Allah ﷻ make the Qur'an a source of guidance, understanding, and peace for all of us, and grant us the ability to read it, understand it, and live by its teachings.

Ameen

— Jalwa Rahmani
